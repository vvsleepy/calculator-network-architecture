Note on framing safety
-----------------------
Every field above that this document could get wrong (a header
mis-parsed, a status misread) is still bounded by the one field that
cannot be wrong: Payload Length at offset 0x04. A receiver reads
exactly that many bytes before it ever looks at what is inside them.
That is why a malformed payload degrades to "we understood the
envelope, not the contents" (-> 400) instead of desynchronising the
whole TCP stream, and it is why a frame of a type the receiver has
never heard of can still be skipped byte-for-byte without guessing.
